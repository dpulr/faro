# Faro press kit

Official assets and product copy for articles, directories and community posts.

## One line

**Spanish:** Faro convierte la barra de menú y el notch del Mac en un centro de control nativo para diagnóstico, automatización, almacenamiento, audio y herramientas cotidianas.

**English:** Faro turns the Mac menu bar and notch into a native control center for diagnostics, automation, storage, audio and everyday tools.

## Short description

Faro is a free, open-source and local-first macOS app. It explains what is slowing the Mac down, turns the notch into a configurable widget matrix, automates layouts and controls through Scenes, monitors connected equipment and provides safe storage cleanup. It has no accounts or telemetry.
